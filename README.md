## Key Research Themes Identified

### 1. AI-Driven Business Intelligence

The literature demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can enhance Business Intelligence architectures by improving analytical capabilities, predictive performance, processing efficiency, and decision support.

### 2. AI and Strategic Decision-Making

AI-enabled analytics can provide organizations with predictive and prescriptive insights that support more informed and timely strategic and operational decisions.

### 3. Integrated Analytics and Decision Support

The literature indicates an increasing connection between Business Intelligence, Business Analytics, Business Process Management, Big Data Analytics, Process Mining, Generative AI, and Decision Support Systems.

### 4. Explainable and Trustworthy AI

P004 introduced Explainable Artificial Intelligence as an important dimension of AI adoption. Transparency, interpretability, auditability, and human oversight are important when AI outputs are used to support decisions.

### 5. Data and Governance

Data quality, governance, security, interoperability, and organizational readiness repeatedly appear as important factors affecting the effectiveness of AI-enabled analytical systems.

Current Research Insight

The literature reviewed so far suggests that the value of AI in organizations extends beyond prediction and automation.

AI can strengthen Business Analytics and decision-making by improving the ability to process complex data, generate analytical insights, support prediction and scenario analysis, and provide decision support.

At the same time, successful AI adoption requires attention to data quality, governance, transparency, explainability, human oversight, and organizational context.
